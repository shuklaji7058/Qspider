TOKENS - Smallest individual unit in program is called as Tokens.
		 It have 6 types -
		 1)Keywords - 
				●Reserved words with predefined meaning, predefined words to perform specific predefined task.
				●Keywords are aware & Known by Compiler.
				●Keywords always written in lowercase.
				●Keywords are always in Finite numbers(50 keywords & 3 reserved words - true,false,null).
				●eg., class,void,static,if,else,throw,assert
				
		 2)Identifiers -
				●Name given to any components and sub-components of Program is called as Identifiers.
				●Any classname, methodname, variablename, enum is also called as Identifiers.
				●Identifiers always given by programmers/users.
				●They are Infinte.
				
				Rules for identifiers -
					●Should not be a keywords.
					●Should not be in numbers.
					●Should not contain any special characters except $ and _ _ with characters.
					●Should not contain space character.
					
				Conventions to name identifiers - 
					(convention mean - Industry standard followed by programmers worldwide)
					●classname - OneTimePassword (Pascal casing)
					●methodname - oneTimePassword (Camal casing)
					●variablename - onetimepassword (lowercase)
					
		 3)Literals - 
				●Whatever Data/value used in code which is given by programmers are called as literals.
				●In literals there are 
				 -number literals, 
				 -Character literals(Length is always in 1, and enclosed in single quote(' ')), 
				 -Boolean literals(true,false), 
				 -String literals(enclosed in double quote("")) are present.
				 
		 4)Operators
		 5)Punctuator/Separator
		 6)Comments
